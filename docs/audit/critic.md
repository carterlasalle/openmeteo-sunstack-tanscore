## 2. Recommendations that would make it worse

### F-27 — Loosen the drizzle hard-block

**Harm:** “usable, expect damp” is a new comfort policy, not a correction proved by a small precipitation total. The suggested soft list includes **56, freezing drizzle**; downgrading that warning without safety evidence is especially hard to justify. The 0.5 mm/h threshold is also a rate, while the inputs include preceding-interval amounts. Adding a 15% radiation floor to Overall turns a genuine hard block into a positive composite and compounds the two-axis confusion.

**Counter-proposal:** retain conservative precipitation blocks unless an explicitly chosen, justified policy changes them. Display radiation dose separately from “no usable window — drizzle”, with the reason visible. Never manufacture usability or alter environmental dose to stop a legacy composite reading zero.

### F-28 — Demote Overall

**Demotion itself is correct**, as required by the legacy-composite invariant. The harmful version is replacing Overall with an unqualified “better” dose/status and discarding feasibility or confidence. A sunny, blocked or unknown-weather site must not acquire a reassuring verdict just because its physical dose is large. F-28 also must not require an exaggerated visual difference between sites: their actual fixed-duration values, not a desired contrast, decide the comparison.

**Counter-proposal:** rank the environmental reference by fixed-duration dose, retain a separate blocked/unknown/usable verdict and reliability cue, and label Local “percentile within this location”. Remove Overall from primary ranking surfaces or keep it only as adjacent-labelled legacy detail. Do not rename the old composite into a new physical score.

### F-26 — Adopt the proposed UV palette

**Harm:** the proposed `#ffd500` has only **1.42:1 contrast against white**. Substituting it into the current 9 px, unoutlined dots makes moderate UV harder to see. Different hex strings also leave the proposed high score green, so they do not solve semantic or colour-vision separation. This is a visibility regression, not proof of a blanket WCAG failure: the numeric UVI is still present.

**Counter-proposal:** retain numerical UVI, add a textual risk-band legend, and use conventional hazard hue families with a sufficiently visible outline/contrast on every row background. Render dose as neutral magnitude rather than “good” green. Check the actual paired indicators in colour-vision simulations, not merely the intersection of two hex lists.

### F-00 — Add a second headline figure

**Harm:** the quoted source field ends in `_wm2`: it is **irradiance**, not an existing integrated J/m² dose. A ratio from one timestamp is not generally a window-dose ratio. Further, local reflection is emitted separately while the named plane value depends on a broadband geometry factor; claiming “on fresh snow” from that value alone misrepresents its scope. The suggested statement about Skin changing incident exposure is false. Two competing headlines would add complexity before resolving those meanings.

**Counter-proposal:** keep one environmental-reference headline. Put an optional, explicitly provisional plane/reflection readout beside its controls, with its real units and time/interval; describe Skin as response/risk context. Promote a same-window plane dose only after its integration, reflected-component treatment and uncertainty are verified. No fabricated “1339 J/m²” example becomes acceptance data.

### Experience §5 — Adopt Alternative A immediately

**Harm:** “every value already exists; this is template work” is false for the shown today/now/next-window state, user-plane dose and collapsed error/staleness handling. Reordering does not fix F-02, conflicting ranking bases, missing schedule filtering or unknown usability. Hiding the evidence behind “Show me why” while presenting “Best window today” more authoritatively can make an untrustworthy answer easier to act on. The existing hero is about the best day in the run, not necessarily today.

**Counter-proposal:** keep the layout initially; first make one visibly scoped answer truthful about location, date, reference basis and usability. Show freshness, blocked/unknown state and a short uncertainty caveat without opening a disclosure. Preserve per-source evidence and offer comfortable/strongest alternatives as labelled context. Evaluate a larger reorder only after those behaviours work; neither A nor C is proven superior by these audit sketches.

## 4. Invariants misread as defects

- **F-00 and functional QA summary item 6:** unchanged environmental dose after Surface/Tilt/Skin changes is correct. The product invariant and contract §2.1.A define a **location reference independent of surface, posture, Fitzpatrick type and personal MMD**. Poor feedback placement is a UI finding; dependence on those controls would be a new physics bug. Flat-plane azimuth irrelevance and zero Lambertian ground view are also correct geometry.
- **F-28:** South Bend Local 95 versus Palisades Local 86 is not a reversed physical ranking. **Local is interpretation against each site’s own history, not comparable across sites.** The wrong behaviour is presenting its legacy composite as a universal ranking, not the within-location normalization itself.
- **F-27:** positive radiation with a null usable window is arguably correct under the existing conservative lying-out precipitation policy. **Radiation and usability are separate axes**; wetness must not subtract photons. The published day retains both dose and the block, so truthful presentation is preferable to an artificial nonzero Overall floor.
- **Functional QA §1, MMD/Skin:** no score/ranking change is not evidence that personalization is dead. MMD is exposure-relative context, not permission for safe exposure; Fitzpatrick is response interpretation, not a multiplier on location radiation. A promised spectrum conversion still needs its own evidence, but context-only controls need not alter the physical answer.

These defenses do **not** excuse unknown weather reading as perfect, or continued primary use of Overall: those violate the stated invariants rather than express them.

## 5. Missing high-value findings

The reports mention stale schema, missing controls and block policy, but none traces the following consequences end to end. These are tests to run, not claims of an exhaustive new QA pass.

1. **Unknown weather becomes perfect usability — live reproduced.** `GET /api/data?location=pacific-palisades&min_temp=50` has a first half-hour with `temperature_2m=null`, `outdoor_feasibility_complete=false`, reason “unknown (missing weather)”, yet `outdoor_feasibility_0_100=100`, `outdoor_blocked=false` and `comfort_band="perfect"`. Daily blocked count is zero. `opportunity.py:119-161,200-213` records missingness without applying it to eligibility/comfort, and `best_fixed_dose_window` reads the blocked flag but not completeness. **Test:** remove temperature or weather code from an otherwise physically complete candidate; require an unknown verdict and exclusion from usable/comfortable ranking while preserving its physical dose. This is stronger evidence than debating drizzle thresholds.

2. **Null usable windows are relabelled as usable — code-confirmed; all-blocked API state reproduced.** With `min_temp=120`, all 14 South Bend days have zero usable windows and positive strongest doses. In `serving.py:634,643-644`, `+null` makes `bestDay()` stop at zero instead of falling through to the strongest dose, while `?? strongStart` and the dose fallback put strongest values under “best usable”. **Test:** apply an all-blocking threshold and require “no usable window”, not a populated usable headline. The live calendar correctly calls its fallback “Strongest 30m”, but the block is only in its description/status; preserve that distinction explicitly across page and subscription. Rendering the all-blocked page was not exercised in this critic pass.

3. **Responses can cross locations/settings — source-supported, latency test not exercised.** `loadData()` commits every successful response to global `DATA`; there is no request-generation check or cancellation. `LOC` and the controls can already describe a later request when an earlier request finishes and renders its old payload, including a calendar URL built from the new controls. **Test:** delay South Bend response A, switch to Palisades B, complete B then A; require that the visible location, payload location and subscription remain B. F-02’s error-only stale banner would not catch this successful-but-wrong response.

4. **Surface reflection needs spectral/energy validation before promotion — code and values confirmed.** `spectral.py:267-296` computes the named plane value with regional broadband albedo, then copies `shortwave_radiation × reflectance × ground view` into **both** reflected UVA and UVB before weighting them. The live fresh-snow/45° request at October 7 14:00 reports reflected delayed-pigmentation irradiance **48.7786 W/m²**, versus environmental **0.63116 W/m²** and named plane **0.87528 W/m²**. QA verifies that fields change, not that these numbers represent their labelled UV bands or a complete user-plane total. **Test:** compare reflected bands with incident UVA/UVB under the declared reflectance/view factors and independently reconcile direct, diffuse and reflected components. Do not just add the separate reflection field to a plane factor that already includes ground bounce.

5. **Nonfinite temperature input silently disables the cold floor — live reproduced.** `GET /api/data?location=south-bend&min_temp=nan` succeeds; the 43.1 °F first hour becomes unblocked, 100% feasible and “perfect”, with the threshold serialized as null. The equivalent finite default 50 °F row is blocked as too cold. **Test:** require 4xx for NaN and both infinities on data and calendar endpoints, without changing the last accepted view. This is the concrete validation hole F-19’s arbitrary range proposal misses.

## 6. The one change to ship first

**Propagate unknown outdoor weather into the usable/comfortable decision, rather than perfect weather.** Make missing essential weather an explicit unknown state in the shared feasibility result, require complete feasibility for usable/comfortable ranking, and show that state instead of a reassuring fit/comfort verdict. Leave environmental irradiance and strongest physical dose intact. Acceptance is the existing live Palisades missing-temperature row no longer reporting 100%/perfect, plus one physically complete candidate with missing temperature/code being excluded from usable ranking without losing its dose.

This is an observed contradiction of an explicit product invariant on a shipped location, not an inferred preference or a proposed aesthetic. It prevents false reassurance and is a prerequisite for any more authoritative answer-first UI. Defer drizzle-policy relaxation, the second physical headline, arbitrary temperature bounds, palette substitution, new sorting/export modes and the A/C layout rewrite. F-02’s visible last-successful-view state and the half-hour UTC correction remain high-priority subsequent work; neither justifies shipping unknown weather as perfect while polishing the answer.
